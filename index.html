Vicar's Message

"Dearly beloved pilgrims, brothers, sisters, and children in Christ,"

It is with immense joy and gratitude to Almighty God that I welcome you to the official web portal of St. Alphonsa Pilgrim Church, Nellyadi, under the Syro-Malabar Catholic Diocese of Belthangady. As a recognized pilgrim center, our shrine is a blessed sanctuary drawing the faithful for prayer, spiritual renewal, and active fellowship.

Following the inspiring life of our patroness St. Alphonsa—our first Indian saint who found holiness in ordinary daily suffering and deep love for the Eucharist—let us walk together in unity, supporting one another through prayer and charitable deeds.

May the Almighty bless your families and pilgrimage with peace, health, and abundant spiritual graces through the intercession of St. Alphonsa.

— Rev. Fr. Mathew Joseph Vettamthadathil, Vicar

Pilgrim Center History & Patron

St. Alphonsa Pilgrim Church, Nellyadi is a renowned pilgrim shrine belonging to the Syro-Malabar Catholic Diocese of Belthangady, Dakshina Kannada, Karnataka. Named after St. Alphonsa, the first canonized saint of India, our parish attracts numerous pilgrims seeking spiritual solace, novena graces, and active charitable outreach.

As a designated pilgrim center in the Diocese of Belthangady, our community gathers regularly for Holy Qurbana, special novenas, pious associations, youth apostolate, and pilgrim hospitality.
